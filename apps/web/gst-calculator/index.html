How GST is calculated

There are two directions, depending on whether your price already includes GST.

Adding GST to a price

GST = price × rate ÷ 100
Total = price + GST
Example: ₹10,000 at 18% → GST ₹1,800 → total ₹11,800

Removing GST from a GST-inclusive amount

Price before GST = total × 100 ÷ (100 + rate)
GST = total − price before GST
Example: ₹11,800 including 18% → price ₹10,000 → GST ₹1,800

A common mistake is to take 18% of the inclusive amount: 18% of ₹11,800 is ₹2,124, which overstates the tax. Always divide by (100 + rate) instead.

CGST + SGST or IGST?

It depends on where the supply goes, not on the rate:

  • Same state (intra-state supply): the GST is split equally into CGST and SGST. At 18% that is 9% + 9%.
  • Different state (inter-state supply): the whole amount is IGST, e.g. 18% IGST.
  • Union territories without a legislature (Chandigarh, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Andaman and Nicobar, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu) use UTGST in place of SGST.
  • Exports of services are zero-rated: under a Letter of Undertaking you charge no GST at all. See export invoices under LUT.

For services to a registered business, the place of supply is usually the client's location, so the first two digits of the client's GSTIN (the state code) tell you which case applies. You can check those with the GSTIN checker.

Current GST rates

Since 22 September 2025, most goods and services fall into two main slabs, 5% and 18%, with a 40% rate for a short list of luxury and sin goods. The older 12% and 28% slabs were largely folded into these. Most professional and IT services, such as software development, design, consulting and marketing, are charged at 18%. Rates for specific items do change, so confirm yours on the CBIC website or with your CA.

Questions

Do freelancers have to charge GST?

Only if you are registered under GST. Registration is generally required once your turnover from services crosses ₹20 lakh in a financial year (₹10 lakh in some special category states). Below that, you can issue a plain invoice without GST. Some cases need registration regardless of turnover, so check with a CA if you are unsure.

Is GST charged before or after a discount?

On the price after any discount shown on the invoice. That discounted figure is the taxable value, and the GST rate applies to it.

How do I show GST on an invoice?

A tax invoice lists the taxable value, the rate and amount of CGST and SGST (or IGST), the SAC or HSN code, your GSTIN and the place of supply. The GST invoice format guide has the full list, or make one here and it is laid out for you.

Why is my CGST and SGST off by one paisa?

When the total tax is an odd number of paise, it cannot be split exactly in half. This calculator gives the extra paisa to SGST. Invoices usually round the final total to the nearest rupee anyway.
