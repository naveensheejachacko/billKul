Export invoice under LUT for services

Working for clients abroad? With a Letter of Undertaking you can invoice them with zero GST. Here's what that takes and what the invoice has to say.

What is an LUT?

A Letter of Undertaking (LUT) is a declaration you file on the GST portal, in form GST RFD-11, saying you'll meet the export rules. Once it's accepted, you can export services without paying IGST. Without an LUT you'd charge 18% IGST, pay it, and then wait for a refund.

  • Filed online on the GST portal, under Services → User Services → Furnish Letter of Undertaking (LUT).
  • No fee, and you get an ARN (acknowledgement number) straight away.
  • Valid for one financial year. File a new one each April.

When does work count as an export of services?

Section 2(6) of the IGST Act sets five conditions. All of them must hold:

  1. You, the supplier, are located in India.
  2. Your client is located outside India.
  3. The place of supply is outside India.
  4. You are paid in convertible foreign exchange, or in rupees where the RBI allows it.
  5. You and the client aren't just two branches of the same entity.

For most remote work, like software, design, writing or consulting for a foreign company, the place of supply is the client's location, so condition 3 follows from condition 2. Some services, such as those tied to property or events in India, have different rules, so check those with your CA.

What to print on an export invoice

Everything a normal GST tax invoice needs, plus:

  • The declaration, in words like “Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of integrated tax”, ideally with your LUT ARN.
  • The client's name and full address, including the country.
  • IGST shown as 0%, or left out, with the place of supply shown as outside India.

Making one in BillKul

  1. Add your GSTIN and LUT ARN in Your details.
  2. In the invoice, set the client to Outside India and enter the country.
  3. Tick Export under LUT. GST drops to 0% and the declaration is printed for you.

BillKul prints amounts in rupees. If you agreed a price in dollars or euros, mention it and the exchange rate in the notes.

Questions

Do I need GST registration to export services?

Exports count as inter-state supplies. Service providers whose turnover is below the ₹20 lakh threshold are generally not required to register just because they export, but you need a GSTIN to file an LUT. If you aren't registered, you simply invoice without GST. Check your situation with a CA.

What happens if I forget to renew the LUT?

An LUT covers one financial year. Exports after it lapses would need IGST paid and refunded later, so file the new one at the start of each April.

Do I still report exports in my GST returns?

Yes. Zero-rated exports are reported in GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B even though no tax is charged on them.

Can I invoice a foreign client in dollars?

Many freelancers state the foreign currency amount on the invoice along with the rupee value. Keep the bank's foreign inward remittance advice as proof that you were paid in foreign exchange.
