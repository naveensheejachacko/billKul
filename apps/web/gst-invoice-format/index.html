What a GST tax invoice must contain

Rule 46 of the CGST Rules lists the details. For a service business, that means:

  1. Your name, address and GSTIN.
  2. An invoice number of up to 16 characters, using only letters, digits, “-” and “/”, unique within the financial year. For example INV/26-27/001.
  3. The date of issue.
  4. The client's name, address and GSTIN, if they are registered.
  5. For an unregistered client and an invoice of ₹50,000 or more: their name and address, with the state name and code.
  6. The SAC code for services (HSN for goods) and a description.
  7. The taxable value, after any discount.
  8. The rate and amount of tax: CGST and SGST (or UTGST) for a client in your state, IGST for a client in another state.
  9. The place of supply with the state name, for inter-state supplies.
  10. Whether tax is payable on reverse charge.
  11. Your signature or digital signature, or that of an authorised person.

Businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore must show at least a 4-digit HSN or SAC on invoices to registered clients; above ₹5 crore, 6 digits. Common SAC codes for freelancers are 998314 for IT design and development, 998313 for IT consulting and 998391 for specialty design.

When to issue it, and how many copies

For services, issue the invoice within 30 days of providing the service. Make two copies: the original for your client and a duplicate for your records. A PDF is fine for both.

Businesses with aggregate turnover above ₹5 crore must also generate an e-invoice (an IRN and signed QR code from the government's Invoice Registration Portal). BillKul doesn't do that step, so it suits freelancers and small businesses below the threshold.

Questions

Can I make a GST invoice in Word or Excel?

Yes, the law cares about the contents, not the software. The risk is maths and missing fields: a wrong IGST versus CGST + SGST choice, or a reused invoice number. A generator that checks these for you avoids the mistakes.

Does an invoice need a stamp or a handwritten signature?

No stamp is required. Rule 46 asks for the signature or digital signature of the supplier or an authorised representative; for PDFs sent by email, many businesses sign digitally or print the name of the signatory.

Can I restart invoice numbers every year?

Yes. Numbers must be consecutive and unique within a financial year (April to March), so most businesses include the year, such as INV/26-27/001, and start again from 001 each April.

Can I put a UPI QR code on a GST invoice?

Yes, and it gets you paid faster: the client scans it and the exact amount is filled in. BillKul prints one automatically when you add your UPI ID.
